The Problem

  • Visually impaired users need AI to navigate their personal spaces.
  • Current tools require sending private photos to massive cloud servers.
  • This is a huge privacy risk and fails without a strong internet connection.

Our Solution

  • 100% Local Edge AI: Zero cloud computing. Absolute privacy.
  • Hands-Free UI: Full Voice-In and Voice-Out interaction.
  • Offline Capability: Works anywhere, anytime.
